CLINICAL ARCHITECTURE

Built around VeGu's 4 documented learning rules

Every screen and printable sheet in Team VeGu enforces the accommodations from his clinical evaluations:

1 · Receptive < Expressive (PLS-5 SS 58 vs. 67)

He uses more language than he understands. Adults must use concise 3–5 word directions paired with a concrete picture or object—never long verbal explanations.

2 · The 8-Second Processing Wait Timer

To break prompt dependence, the app includes an interactive 8-second countdown bar. Adults give the cue once, then wait silently for 8 seconds before offering a gesture or model.

3 · 3–5 Min Sensory Prime Before Table Work

His OT confirmed vestibular movement + heavy work raises persistence. Every lesson starts with a NEEDS FIRST box (trampoline jumps, heavy push/pull, chewy tool).

4 · Strictly Capped at 5 Trials (Rule of 5)

Fine-motor fatigue and refusal start after Trial 4–5. Every worksheet and data logger has exactly 5 trials and 5 gold stars so he always finishes on a win.

Structure prevents frustration before it starts.

HOW AT-HOME LESSONS WORK

A predictable 10-minute loop any adult can run

Short, bite-sized sessions that fit easily into morning routines or therapy blocks:

1

Regulate (3–5m)

Trampoline jumps, spinning, swing, or heavy laundry basket push + chewy tool.

2

First–Then (30s)

Point to Printable Sheet 1: "First 5 trials → Then Sensory Table" + start timer.

3

5 Trials (2m)

Deliver 3–5 word cue, run 8s wait timer, award 1 gold star per trial. Stop at 5!

4

1-Tap Log (15s)

Tap Prompt Level (I, V, G, Vb, P) for Trials 1–5 on phone.

5

Reinforce (3–5m)

Immediate celebratory transition to sensory table, pretend kitchen, or song.

Total seated demand: 1 to 2 minutes—aligned directly with his IEP Daily Living Goal 1.

DATA THAT MATTERS

Measuring prompt fading, not just pass/fail

A skill isn't mastered until VeGu does it independently across people and settings. Every trial logs one of 6 levels:

🟢 I · Independent

Completes within 8 seconds of natural verbal direction—no gesture or physical help. Counts toward the 80% across 3 sessions IEP criterion.

🔵 V / G · Visual or Gestural

Responds using the printed visual card, GO/STOP dot, or adult point. Shows emerging comprehension while physical prompts fade out.

🟠 Vb / P · Verbal Repeat or Physical

Needs repeated cue or hand-over-hand assistance. Tracked transparently so OT, SLP, and ABA can adjust task difficulty.

Our stacked bar charts prove prompt fading visually at every 9-week IEP review.
